# Relay

Your production team, together. One producer owns the result. The client decides on each cut. The crew only moves a task when the handoff is clear.

Open the app: https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/princenhammai/relay/blob/main/index.html

This build is the Luma Autumn Launch demo. One film, six cutdowns, delivery 21 October. The team and project are sample data.

## Who does what

| Person | Role | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| Nora Chen | Producer | Scope, schedule, crew, final delivery. The only point person. |
| Leo Park | Creative director | Idea, visual direction, storyboard quality. |
| Ari Nguyen | Researcher and scriptwriter | Hooks, script, claims. |
| Minh Tran | Camera and lighting | Shot list, shoot day, original footage. |
| Sam Rivera | Editor, sound and color | Main film, six cutdowns, mix and grade. |
| Maya Patel | Motion designer | Titles, end card, brand graphics. |

The client does not manage the crew. The client watches a cut, approves it, or requests a change.

## What to do

**Client**

1. Open Home. The top card is the only thing waiting on you.
2. Open that cut in Review. A comment is feedback. Approve locks that cut only.
3. Request changes if something is wrong. Nora turns it into a handoff. Do not decide in Messages.
4. Send footage in Files. Nora verifies it before the editor gets it.
5. Use Messages only to talk to Nora. No agent replies.

**Nora**

1. Stay the single owner from brief to delivery.
2. Switch to Production view. Every card needs an input, an owner, and a next handoff.
3. Do not mark Shoot done until files are verified. Shoot day is 14 October and is still planned.
4. Send the client update yourself. Drafts in Apps do not send.

**Crew**

1. Take only the task with your name on the board.
2. Minh works from Shoot plan and Shot list. Wrap hands originals to Sam.
3. Ari owns the script. Leo owns the storyboard. Maya takes the end-card plate.
4. Sam cuts only after Nora assigns verified footage.

## Screens

**Client view:** Home, Review, Storyboard, Files, Messages, Team, Apps.

**Production view:** Board, Shoot plan, Shot list, Script, Storyboard, Review, Files, Messages, Team, AI desk, Apps.

- Home is the next decision plus the seven deliverables.
- Review is the decision. Notes sit on a timestamp. Approval does not cover the other cuts.
- Storyboard is frames before the cut. It is not approval.
- Shoot plan is the 14 October day board, call through wrap.
- Shot list is the four setups: frame, lens, time.
- Script is action and voiceover. Claims stay with Ari.
- Board is Complete, In progress, Next.
- AI desk drafts captions from approved cuts only, and only when someone runs it.
- Apps carry files, dates, and the brief. Relay keeps the decision.

## Rules

- One cut, one decision.
- Two feedback channels are not allowed. Review decides. Messages coordinate.
- AI does not listen in Messages and does not reply.
- Slack posts a handoff. Frame.io exports notes. Neither one approves a cut.
- Drive folders wait on Nora before Sam sees them.
